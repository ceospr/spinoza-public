Reading these results

Profit factor compares gross realized dollar gains with gross realized dollar losses in the eligible closed-trade history. It is unavailable when the source does not support that comparison or the denominator is zero.

Trade returns reflect matched fills. Fees, dividends and borrow costs are excluded unless recorded in the source. Breakeven trades are distinct from wins and losses. An unavailable metric is not zero.
