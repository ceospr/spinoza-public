    Conditional estimates

    Published final estimates depend on the research available at the stated date. They are not forecasts or probabilities of investment returns.

    Separate estimate types

    Intrinsic equity estimates, relative pricing comparisons and income scenarios answer different questions. An income scenario does not establish total equity value or property NAV.

    Unavailable estimates
